What this covers

This is the explicit consent GeoPocket asks of users in Türkiye for personalised ads in the free version and for the transfer abroad that it needs, under Turkish data protection law (KVKK). It is kept separate from the privacy policy. The binding version is the Turkish açık rıza metni. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, consent is asked through Google's consent message instead.

Controller: Vecihi Asım Kartoğlu (individual developer), Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: geopocket.info@gmail.com.

Data and purpose

If you agree, Google LLC's ad service (Google AdMob) processes your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device details and ad interactions to choose ads by your interests, measure ad performance and limit how often ads are shown. Google may combine this with data it holds from other apps and sites.

Transfer abroad and its risks

The data is transferred to Google's servers abroad, mainly in the USA. The risks: there is no adequacy decision of the Turkish authority for these countries, so protection may differ from Türkiye's; authorities there may be able to access the data under their laws; and exercising your rights against a company abroad may be harder.

Consent is optional

You don't have to agree. Without consent every feature of GeoPocket works the same; ads are simply not chosen by your interests. With Pro no ads are shown at all.

Giving and withdrawing consent

Consent is given on a dedicated screen in the app (on iPhone, iOS's App Tracking Transparency prompt also has to be allowed), with "I agree" and "I don't agree" offered equally. Withdraw any time without giving a reason: in the app Settings › Permissions › Personalised ads; on iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; on Android Settings › Google › All services › Ads.
