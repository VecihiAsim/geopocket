Contact us

Questions, bug reports and ideas: geopocket.info@gmail.com. We usually answer within 2 working days.

It helps to include your phone model, iOS or Android version, GeoPocket version (Settings › About), what you did and what happened, and a screenshot if you can. If you send a sample file, remove personal data such as owners' names first.

How do I open a file?

  • In GeoPocket, use Files › Add file and pick a file from your phone, iCloud Drive or Google Drive.
  • In email, WhatsApp or another app, use Share › GeoPocket.
  • Open a Shapefile as one ZIP holding the .shp, .dbf, .shx and .prj files.

My data shows in the wrong place

If the file holds no coordinate system (most NCZ, DWG, DXF and CSV files), choose it from ⋮ › Coordinate System at the top right of the map, for example ITRF96 TM30. The choice is remembered for that file. If the basemap still doesn't line up, check the datum (ITRF96 or ED50) and the zone's central meridian.

How do I restore Pro?

On a phone signed in to the same Apple or Google account, open the GeoPocket Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Lifetime Pro and active subscriptions come back.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › GeoPocket › Cancel subscription. Android: Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › GeoPocket › Cancel subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google take the payments, so they handle refunds: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play through a refund request. See the Terms of use.

Ads and permissions

The free version shows ads. An ad never appears the moment a file opens, and two ads are at least 90 seconds apart. You can turn personalised ads off any time as described in Consent for personalised ads. Pro shows no ads.

How do I delete my data?

Everything GeoPocket keeps is on your phone: delete files from the Files screen, or delete the app to remove all settings. To have your emails to us deleted, just ask. See the Privacy policy.
