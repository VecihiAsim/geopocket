Who is responsible

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use the GeoPocket mobile app and this website. The controller is Vecihi Asım Kartoğlu (individual developer), Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: geopocket.info@gmail.com.

For users in Türkiye the Turkish text, the KVKK aydınlatma metni, is the governing version. Consent for personalised ads is asked separately; see Consent for personalised ads.

In short

  • No account: you don't sign up, and no name, email or phone number is asked.
  • The files you open are processed on your phone and are never sent to the developer or to a server. The developer runs no server.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google; some technical data goes from your device to Google.
  • Pro is bought through Apple or Google; your payment details never reach the developer.
  • If you turn on a basemap, map tiles are downloaded from OpenStreetMap or OpenTopoMap.

Data inside your files (for example parcel owners' names) is opened, shown and converted only on your phone at your request; the developer does not access, collect or keep it. If your files hold other people's personal data, you or your organisation are responsible for it.

The app does not ask for location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts or photos permission.

Who receives data

  • Google LLC (USA) and affiliates, ad serving through Google AdMob; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Apple and Google as app stores, for payments and subscriptions, as independent controllers.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (UK) and OpenTopoMap (Germany), map tiles, only when a basemap is on.
  • Public authorities, where the law requires it.

Data is never sold.

International transfers

These providers process data outside your country, including in the USA. For users in the EEA and UK, Google relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. For users in Türkiye, transfers for personalised ads rest on explicit consent after being told the risks (KVKK 9/6-a), and the other transfers are necessary to perform the contract with you (KVKK 9/6-b).

How long data is kept

  • Your settings, the permissions you gave and your Pro status stay on your phone and are deleted with the app.
  • Support emails are kept for at most 2 years after the matter is closed.
  • Data held by Google and Apple follows their own retention rules.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing, data portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing rests on consent you may withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier processing. Write to geopocket.info@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days, free of charge. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority; in the EU, your local supervisory authority).

Changing ad permission: in the app, Settings › Ad privacy choices (where shown); on iPhone, Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; on Android, Settings › Google › All services › Ads.

Children

GeoPocket is made for professionals and adults working with maps, surveys and CAD/GIS files. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, analytics or ads. GitHub, Inc. may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

Changes

This policy is updated when the app or the law changes. The current version is always on this page; important changes are also announced in the app.
